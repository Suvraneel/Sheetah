Sheetah: open the whole file

A spreadsheet that scrolls millions of rows and speaks SQL, running entirely in your browser. Open CSV, Excel (.xlsx), JSON and Parquet files of any size your computer can hold; filter, sort, pivot and chart them; and write SQL when you want to. Sheetah runs the DuckDB database inside the page, so your files are read from your disk and never uploaded.

  • Smooth scrolling from one to ten million rows (Excel stops at 1,048,576)
  • Point-and-click filters, sorting, formula columns and column profiles that write readable SQL
  • Pivot tables and quick charts computed over every row
  • Clean numbers and dates stored as text, look up values from another table, stack monthly exports
  • Compare two tables, and anonymize personal data before you share a file
  • Export to Excel (.xlsx), CSV or Parquet
  • Works offline, installs as an app, no account and no upload
